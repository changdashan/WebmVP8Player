ffmpeg -f dshow -i video="Integrated Webcam" -f webm -r 20 -s 800x450 -c:v libvpx -b:v 1M -g 10 http://127.0.0.1:8081/password

To find the name of your local webcam, run the following command on your cmd console:
ffmpeg -list_devices true -f dshow -i dummy

In the output, identify the lines with (vidoe) an (audio). The double quoted names are the name of your video and audio device names:
[in#0 @ 000001c2c226f1c0] "Integrated Webcam" (video)
[in#0 @ 000001c2c226f1c0] "Microphone (Realtek(R) Audio)" (audio)
